Template for the `## Handoff` section of `.pavii/PAVII.md`.

## Handoff

The moving picture: where the work stands right now. Rewrite it as things
change rather than adding to the end.

### State

One or two sentences: what is being worked on and how far it has got.

### Done

- What is finished and verified. Say how it was verified.

### Next

1. The most useful thing to do next, specifically enough to start on.
2. Then this.

### Files

- `path/to/file` — what changed in it and why.

### Gotchas

- Anything surprising that cost time and would cost it again: a non-obvious
  failure mode, a command that must be run a particular way, a trap in the
  environment.

### Blocked

- What cannot proceed, and what would unblock it. Leave this out when nothing
  is blocked.

### Open questions

- Anything that needs a decision from the user before the work can continue.

---

Say what is actually true, including what is unfinished or broken. A handoff
that overstates progress costs the next session more than it saves.
