# Architecture Record

Durable facts about this project. Keep it short. Delete what stops being true.

## What this is

One or two sentences: what the project does and who it is for.

## Goals and constraints

- What it must do well.
- What it must not do, or cannot do — platform limits, compliance, budget,
  compatibility it has to keep.

## How it fits together

The main parts and what each is responsible for. Name real directories and
entry points so the next session can find them. Skip anything obvious from the
directory listing.

## Decisions

| Decision | Why | What else was considered |
| -------- | --- | ------------------------ |
|          |     |                          |

Record a decision here when reversing it would be expensive, or when someone
would otherwise undo it without knowing why it was made.

## Risks and debt

- Known weak spots, shortcuts taken deliberately, and what would go wrong if
  they are ignored.

---

Fill in only what you have evidence for. Leave a section out rather than
guessing at it.
